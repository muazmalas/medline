Status source of truth
- Patient order and procurement statuses: docs/DOMAIN_SPECIFICATION.md plus current controller transition logic.
- Delivery statuses: current DeliveryController and current seed invariant set. The active sequence is available -> claimed -> pickup_started -> in_transit -> arrived -> delivered. Successful pickup PIN verification moves directly from pickup_started to in_transit; there is no separate driver "start delivery" action.
- Organization, subscription, document, complaint, and user statuses: current controllers and domain specification.
