Communication job
By the end, the university committee should understand that MedLine is a coherent, role-secured medication logistics platform - not a collection of screens - because every patient order, pharmacy decision, warehouse replenishment, driver handoff, notification, and administrator intervention follows an auditable server-controlled workflow.

Central takeaway
MedLine coordinates the complete medication journey with role-specific experiences, road-aware delivery, traceable stock, two-step handoff verification, and server-enforced consistency across web and mobile clients.
